FAQ

Why does this differ from my calculator?

Most calculators and JavaScript itself store integers as double-precision floats, which are only exact up to 2^53. Here everything goes through BigInt, so 20-digit IDs and permission masks stay accurate.

What does “auto” detect?

A 0x/0b/0o prefix selects hex/binary/octal; otherwise a number containing a-f is read as hex, and anything else as decimal.

What is the grouped bit view for?

Reading flags and masks. Grouping into nibbles (4 bits) lets you map each group to one hex digit; bytes (8 bits) match how the value is laid out in memory.
