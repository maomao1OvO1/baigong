FAQ

Why is the financial form needed at all?

In Chinese accounting, amounts on receipts and contracts are written in the uppercase form because 一 can be altered into 二 or 十 with a pen, while 壹 cannot. It is a tamper-resistance measure, not decoration.

How large a number can it handle?

Up to 15 digits. Beyond that floating-point precision ends, and the tool says so instead of returning a wrong answer.
