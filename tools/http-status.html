FAQ

What actually separates 4xx from 5xx?

4xx means the request was wrong (bad params, not signed in, too many calls); 5xx means the machinery on my side failed. That line tells you whether to fix code or add capacity.

Why are 418, 499 and 599 marked non-official?

They aren't in any RFC: 418 is an April Fools' joke, 499 was invented by Nginx, 599 shows up in proxies. Flagged so you don't think you misremembered.
