FAQ

Why convert through HSL?

Because the color wheel only exists in HSL. Rotating “180°” in RGB is meaningless — all three channels would shift linearly and you would get a muddy color, not the complement.

How do I use the lightness ladder?

Pick a middle step for the base, lighter steps for hover and backgrounds, darker ones for pressed and borders. Keeping the hue and saturation fixed is what makes the set look deliberate.
