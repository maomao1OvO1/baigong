FAQ

Why does my pattern show an error?

The message comes straight from the JavaScript engine, and it is usually accurate: an unclosed group, an unmatched bracket, or a quantifier with nothing before it. Check the text after the message — it names the position.

Why does a match with no g flag stop after the first hit?

That is how regular expressions work: without g the engine returns the first match only. Turn on the global flag to list every match.

What are $1, $2 in the output?

Capture groups: the parts of the pattern written in parentheses. Use them in the replacement field as $1, $2 — or $& for the whole match.
