FAQ

Is MD5 still safe to use?

Not for security. MD5 and SHA-1 collisions are practical, so never use them for signatures or password storage. They remain fine as plain checksums (comparing two files, cache keys, legacy APIs that demand MD5).

Why do I get a different hash than a website told me?

Nine times out of ten it is trailing whitespace or line endings. Copy the exact bytes: a trailing newline changes the digest completely.

Can I hash a file here?

This page hashes text. For files, open the file and paste its contents, or use your system's md5sum / certutil command.
