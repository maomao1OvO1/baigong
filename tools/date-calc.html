FAQ

Why does the day count differ from a calendar app?

Time zones and daylight saving. This tool compares the two dates as calendar days in UTC, so a DST switch cannot turn the result into a fraction of a day.

What is an ISO week number?

The ISO-8601 week: weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week containing the year's first Thursday. That is why 1 January can belong to week 52 or 53 of the previous year.
