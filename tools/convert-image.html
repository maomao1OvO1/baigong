    FAQ

    Which way should I convert?

    For photos: JPG or WebP (much smaller). For graphics, screenshots and anything with transparency: PNG. WebP is the best all-rounder if your target supports it.

    What happens to transparency?

    PNG and WebP keep it. JPG cannot — those areas are filled with white, which is why converting a logo to JPG usually looks wrong.

    Can I convert many files at once?

    Yes, and it's unlimited — the conversion runs on your own device.
