    Questions people actually ask

    How much smaller will they get?

    Typically 50–85% off a JPEG photo at 72% quality. It depends on the original — screenshots and PNGs sometimes shrink a lot more.

    Does it work on a phone?

    Yes, and phone photos are the case it helps most: 12-megapixel shots often drop from 4 MB to a few hundred KB.

    Why does the quality slider do nothing with PNG?

    PNG is lossless, so there's no quality to trade away. For photos, pick JPEG or WebP.

    Is there a batch limit?

    No. Pick 300 files if you want. It's your processor doing the work, so it's about a second or two per image.

    Do I need to keep the tab open?

    Yes — the compression happens on your device, so the page has to be running. Nothing is uploaded, so there's no server job to come back to.
