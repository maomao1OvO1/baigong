    FAQ

    How much smaller will they get?

    Typically 50–85% off a JPEG photo at 72% quality. It depends on the original — screenshots and PNGs sometimes shrink much more.

    Why does the quality slider do nothing with PNG?

    PNG is lossless, so there is no quality to trade away. For photos, pick JPEG or WebP.

    Is there a batch limit?

    No. It's your processor doing the work, so expect about a second per image.
