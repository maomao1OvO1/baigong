FAQ

What's the difference between MB and MiB?

This converter uses decimal units (1 KB = 1000 bytes), which is what disk makers and most cloud services mean. Operating systems often show binary (1024) — so a “500 GB” drive shows as ~465 GB in Windows.

Does it do temperature?

Yes — pick the temperature category and it shows Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin together.

Why show all units instead of just two?

Because you usually want to compare three or four at once, and picking two dropdowns is pure friction.
