FAQ

Seconds or milliseconds?

Both. 10 digits is treated as seconds, 13 as milliseconds — that's the convention in virtually every API.

Why do I see two times?

Because servers store UTC while you think in local time. Showing both is how you spot an off-by-hours bug in one glance.

What date formats can I type?

2026-10-11 12:30:45, 2026/10/11, 2026-10-11T12:30 and similar — slashes are accepted and converted.
