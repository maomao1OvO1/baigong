    FAQ

    Why does the host look different from what I typed?

    Browsers and new URL() apply the WHATWG standard: international domains are converted to punycode and illegal characters are percent-encoded. That is exactly what gets sent on the wire, so seeing it here is useful.

    What does the decode switch do?

    It shows %E4%B8%AD as the character it stands for, and turns + into a space — the way a form submission would be read. Turn it off to see the raw string.
