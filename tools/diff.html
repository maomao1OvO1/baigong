FAQ

How do I put two texts in one box?

Separate them with a line containing only ===. Create a few that way and you can diff several pairs in a row.

Why is a line marked as changed when it looks identical?

Invisible characters: a trailing space, a non-breaking space, or Windows line endings. That is exactly the kind of thing a diff is good at catching.

Is there a size limit?

Yes — around 4000 lines per side. Beyond that the comparison needs too much memory and is refused instead of freezing your tab.
