FAQ

Does the day field OR with the weekday field?

Yes, in standard Vixie cron. 0 0 1 * 1 means “the 1st of the month **or** every Monday”, not “the 1st when it is also a Monday”. This trips people up constantly.

Are 0 and 7 both Sunday?

In the weekday field, yes — both are accepted, and this tool normalises 7 to 0 so the explanation stays consistent.

What time zone is used?

Your device's local time zone, which is what matters when you debug a job that runs on your own machine. Servers usually run UTC, so double-check against the server clock.
