FAQ

UUID v4 or v7 — which should I use?

New projects: v7. It starts with a millisecond timestamp, so IDs sort by creation time and your database index stays happy. v4 is pure random: fine as an identifier, but random inserts fragment B-tree indexes at scale.

ULID vs UUID v7 — they look similar?

Both are time-sortable. ULID is 26 characters of Crockford Base32 (no dashes, easier to read and shorter in URLs); UUID v7 is the standard 36-character RFC form most databases have a native type for.

Are these IDs safe for secrets?

They are strongly random, but an ID is not a password: it is usually stored and logged somewhere. For tokens use a dedicated secret generator.
