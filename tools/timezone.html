FAQ

Why does New York show -4 sometimes and -5 other times?

Daylight saving. US Eastern is UTC-4 from March to November and UTC-5 otherwise. This tool picks the right one for the moment you entered.

Which time zone is my input in?

Your device's by default — or pick a source zone below, handy when a log line is timestamped in Tokyo time.
