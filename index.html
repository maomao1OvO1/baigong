    So what's the catch?

    There isn't one. Serving these tools costs us almost nothing, because there is no server doing the work — your browser already knows how to compress a JPEG, convert a format and format JSON. Sites that make you upload are paying for machines to process your files, and that bill is exactly why they cap you, watermark you, or slow you down until you subscribe. We skipped that part: no accounts to sell, no data to broker, no tracking pixels.

    How do I know you're not lying?

    Don't take our word for it — open any tool, turn off your Wi-Fi, and keep using it. It still works, because nothing was ever being sent. Your files stay on your device; that isn't a promise we're making, it's how the page is built.
