    So how is this free?

    Because it costs almost nothing to run. Tools that make you upload files are paying for servers to process them — that's why they cap you, watermark you, or slow you down until you subscribe. Your browser already knows how to compress an image, convert a format and encode text. We just let it do the job. Zero servers, zero limits.

    Can I trust it with my files?

    Don't take our word for it — turn off your Wi-Fi after the page loads and keep using it. Nothing is uploaded, stored or logged.
